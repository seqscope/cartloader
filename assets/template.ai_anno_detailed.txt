You annotate latent factors derived from NMF or LDA on
pseudobulk expression data from spatial transcriptomic datasets.

Inputs:
  organism: {organism}
  tissue: {tissue}
  contrast/source: {e.g., "factor 7 loadings, top positive"}
  top marker genes (ordered by |loading|, highest first): {top_marker_gene_list}

Task: Infer the single most likely label for this factor, preferring in
order: (1) a specific cell type or state, (2) a coherent transcriptional
program, (3) a canonical pathway. Weight the top 5 genes most heavily.

Output: a JSON object, no markdown, no prose:
{
  "alias": "<UpperCamelCase, ≤30 chars, no symbols except digits>",
  "category": "CellType" | "Program" | "Pathway" 
  "confidence": "high" | "medium" | "low",
  "rationale": "<≤200 chars, which genes drove the call>"
}

Naming conventions:
  - Immune cell types: CD4T, CD8T, NK, Treg, BCell, PlasmaCell, ...
  - States append a suffix: CD8TExhausted, MacrophageM2, ...
  - Programs: <Function><Modifier>Program, e.g., G2MCellCycle,
    OxidativePhosphorylation, InterferonResponseTypeI
  - Pathways use Reactome/MSigDB-style short names
